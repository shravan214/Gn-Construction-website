From the soil up

Baramati sits on Deccan-trap basalt with a heavy black-cotton layer across Kasba, MIDC and the Bypass corridor. A two-bore soil test is non-negotiable for us on every plot - the foundation design and steel schedule change with the depth at which we hit murrum.

02

MIDC vs Kasba - two different briefs

MIDC plots are tighter, often 1,200–2,500 sqft, with a 1.5 m side margin and a single-window BMC sanction. Kasba plots are larger, with more flexibility on setbacks and a higher FAR for corner plots. We plan the brief, scope and material list differently for each.

Planning Considerations

Differences in plot size, setbacks, and FAR requirements necessitate distinct approaches to design briefs, material selection, and regulatory compliance for MIDC and Kasba projects.

03

What we handle in-house

We handle soil test, architectural plan, structural drawing, BMC / Panchayat sanction, RCC execution, brickwork, plaster, flooring, modular kitchen and interiors — all managed by a single site engineer and covered under one quotation. There are no sub-contracted layers involved.
