How we work

The brief, in sections.

01

The three kinds of renovation we do

  • One: interior-only – modular kitchen, false ceiling, wardrobes, bathrooms, paint.
  • Two: structural – adding floors, removing walls, building a new stair, opening up a floor plan.
  • Three: full redevelopment – knock down, rebuild.

We do all three, with the right team for each.

02

The handover and after

On handover, you get a 1-year defect-liability commitment on all interior work and a 10-year workmanship warranty on structural and waterproofing. If something we did fails in that window, we come back and fix it - no invoice, no debate.

The questions we get most.

How much does a 2 BHK interior renovation cost in Pune?

+

₹8–18 lakh for a complete interior makeover (modular kitchen, false ceiling, wardrobes, bathrooms, paint, electrical). We share a written, line-item quote after a site visit.

Can you add a floor to my existing bungalow?

+

Yes, with a structural feasibility study first. Most Pune and Baramati bungalows can take a G+1 addition if the existing structure was designed for it.
