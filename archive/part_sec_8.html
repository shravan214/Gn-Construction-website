How we build

Five movements. One engineer throughout.

The same engine runs behind every Arsa project — a soil test, an architect, a structural consultant, a written line-item quotation, and a single site engineer from line one to handover.

01

Listen

Your plot, your family, the things that cannot move. A 90-minute site and brief visit, in person or via video.

02

Shape

Plans, three elevations, a 3D walkthrough, and an honest route through permissions and approvals.

03

Align

A written, line-item quotation, a milestone schedule, and the material spec - locked before any work begins.

04

Build

RCC, brickwork, finish and interiors - under one site engineer, on a fixed schedule, with a written delay-penalty clause.

05

Handover

As-built drawings, material test certificates, manufacturer warranties, and a 1-year defect-liability commitment.
