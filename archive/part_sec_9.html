What is the cost of constructing a bungalow in Pune in 2026?

Standard finish: ₹2,400–₹2,900 per sqft. Premium finish (marble, premium sanitaryware, full interiors): ₹3,300–₹4,500 per sqft. We share a written, line-item quotation before any commitment.

How long does a 2,000 sqft bungalow take in Pune or Baramati?

8–10 months for a standard G+1 bungalow, on a typical plot. We share a milestone schedule at contract and stick to it - with a written delay-penalty clause.

Do you take bungalow projects in Pune PMC, PCMC and PMRDA areas?

Yes. We file for permission in PMC, PCMC, PMRDA, BMC, Talegaon Dabhade MC and local Gram Panchayats, depending on which authority covers your plot.

What is included in your turnkey quotation?

Architectural plan, structural design, soil test, building-permission file, RCC and brickwork, plaster, flooring, doors and windows, electrical and plumbing, false ceiling, modular kitchen, wardrobes, paint, compound wall and external works - all line-itemed.

What materials do you use?

OPC 53 grade cement, fresh-batch, factory-sealed. Fe 500D TMT steel, BIS-certified, manufacturer test certificate per lot. Wire-cut clay bricks or AAC blocks per structural design. M20 designed-mix concrete, cube-tested every 30 cum.
